Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about appointments, doctors, and teleconsultations

How quickly can I speak with a specialist?

For Virtual Video Consultations, you can connect with a verified specialist within 10 to 15 minutes. For In-Clinic hospital visits, your appointment slot is pre-confirmed with guaranteed zero waiting time at the clinic reception.

Are your doctors verified and credentialed?

Yes, 100% of our consulting physicians and surgeons are Medical Council of India (MCI) credentialed with an average of 15+ years of clinical practice in premier tertiary hospitals such as Apollo, Fortis, AIIMS, and Medanta.

Will I receive a valid digital medical prescription?

Yes. Following your consultation, your doctor issues an ABDM-compliant, digitally signed e-prescription complete with a QR verification code. It is valid across all pharmacies, diagnostic laboratories, and health insurance providers across India.

Is free follow-up included with my consultation?

Yes! Every consultation includes a complimentary 7-day digital follow-up window. You can ask follow-up questions, share test report updates, and check recovery progress with your specialist at no extra charge.
